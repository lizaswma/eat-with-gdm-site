Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. There are no accounts or sign-in. Everything you log is stored on your device.

Where does the carb information come from?
Food search looks up carb and fiber amounts in the USDA's public FoodData Central database. Only the food name you type is sent.

Can I share my log with my care team?
Yes. From History, use the share button to export the days you choose as a CSV or PDF.

Is this medical advice?
No. Eat with GDM is a logging and general-education tool. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. Always follow the guidance of your healthcare provider.
